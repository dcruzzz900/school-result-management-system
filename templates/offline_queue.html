Offline Queue

When you save one of the offline-capable forms (Add Student, Add Teacher, Add Class, Add Subject, Assign Subjects, Enter Scores, Take Roll Call, or Update Comments) while this device has no connection, it's saved here on this device and sent automatically the next time you're back online. This only tracks items saved on this device, in this browser — it isn't shared with other devices or synced anywhere until it's sent.
